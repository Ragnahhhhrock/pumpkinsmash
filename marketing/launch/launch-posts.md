## LinkedIn (image: linkedin_1200x627.png)
Just launched: Pumpkin Smash 🎃

A small side project that went from idea to live game in days: a free, mobile-first Halloween tap game at pumpkinsmash.me.

Under the hood it's deliberately simple — a static site with no build step, hosted on Cloudflare Pages, with serverless functions powering a global smash counter and a world high score table.

It was a fun reminder that a focused idea, a tight scope and a hard deadline (Halloween) can get a product shipped.

Give it a smash and tell me your score: https://pumpkinsmash.me
